## Certification Requirements

### Certificate of Completion (80% requirement)
To earn a Certificate of Completion, you must successfully complete 80% of the hands-on exercises according to the provided instructions.

**Required Exercises:**
- ✅ Chapter 1: Audio data loading and preprocessing exercise
- ✅ Chapter 2: Pipeline implementation for audio classification and ASR
- ✅ Chapter 4: Music genre classification project (partial completion acceptable)
- ✅ Chapter 5: ASR fine-tuning or evaluation exercise (partial completion acceptable)
- ✅ Chapter 6: TTS implementation exercise (partial completion acceptable)
- ✅ Chapter 7: One integrated application project (partial completion acceptable)

### Certificate of Honors (100% requirement)
To earn a Certificate of Honors, you must successfully complete 100% of all hands-on exercises according to the provided instructions.

**Required Exercises (All):**
- ✅ Chapter 1: Complete audio data analysis and preprocessing
- ✅ Chapter 2: All pipeline implementations (classification, ASR, TTS)
- ✅ Chapter 4: Complete music genre classifier with demo
- ✅ Chapter 5: Full ASR fine-tuning and evaluation project
- ✅ Chapter 6: Complete TTS system with evaluation
- ✅ Chapter 7: All three integrated applications (speech-to-speech, voice assistant, meeting transcription)

## Certification Process

### Step 1: Self-Assessment
Review your completed exercises against the requirements:
- Check that all required code runs without errors
- Verify that outputs match expected results
- Ensure proper documentation and explanations are included
- Confirm that demonstrations/demos work as intended

### Step 2: Project Documentation
Create a portfolio showcasing your work:
- **GitHub repository**: Organize your projects with clear README files
- **Hugging Face Spaces**: Deploy interactive demos for your applications
- **Model uploads**: Share fine-tuned models on Hugging Face Hub
- **Documentation**: Write clear explanations of your implementations

### Step 3: Certification Application
Submit your certification request through the official course platform:
- Provide links to your completed projects
- Include a brief reflection on your learning journey
- Demonstrate understanding of key concepts through project explanations
- Show practical application of audio AI techniques
